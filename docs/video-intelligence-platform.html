Product and architecture overview01 / 08
02 — The problem

Video is rich, but most of its intelligence is trapped inside a timeline.

Organizations have more video than their teams can watch, understand, compare, or reuse.
Creative knowledge disappears.Teams repeatedly reinvent hooks, pacing, CTAs, and brand decisions already present in prior campaigns.
Events are hard to connect.A single incident may span multiple cameras, clips, people, objects, and locations.
Search is not enough.Users need relationships, timelines, patterns, and the exact evidence behind an answer.
Generative answers need trust.Every recommendation should be traceable to a source video and timestamp.

Production deployment model07 / 08
03 — The bigger problem

Three product layers. One compounding foundation.

1

Understand video

Analyze segments, people, objects, actions, speech, text, style, topics, and relationships.

Foundation: searchable, time-coded video intelligence.
2

Act on the intelligence

Generate marketing briefs and templates, answer questions, compare examples, and validate brand rules.

Workflow: turn past work into reusable decisions and content.
3

Discover patterns across domains

Re-use the same graph and agent primitives for security incidents, investigations, and operational vigilance.

Platform: one intelligence layer, multiple vertical products.
