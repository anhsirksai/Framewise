Product and architecture overview01 / 09
The problem

Rich media is full of answers. Its intelligence is trapped in a timeline.

Organizations — and households — have more recorded evidence than anyone can watch, compare, or find again.
Creative knowledge disappears.Teams reinvent hooks, pacing, CTAs, and brand decisions already present in prior campaigns.
Events are hard to connect.A single incident may span multiple cameras, clips, people, objects, and locations.
Search is not enough.Users need relationships, timelines, patterns, and the exact evidence behind an answer.
Generative answers need trust.Every recommendation should be traceable to a source document and timestamp.

The product

HSA Receipt Recall: fetch a bill from 20 years ago with one search.

HSA reimbursements have no deadline. The receipt that proves your claim shouldn’t have one either.
Capturebills, receipts, EOBs

Retrieveask in plain language
The problem.You can reimburse an HSA expense years — even decades — after paying it, tax-free. Almost no one keeps a searchable record that long, so the tax savings are left on the table.
The product.Every medical bill and receipt is ingested, understood, and persisted in the evidence graph with full provenance. Ask “find my dental bill from 2006” and get the exact document back.
Why it works here.The same pipeline that makes video searchable — index, embed, persist — makes a 20-year-old receipt retrievable in a single query.
Demo question.“Fetch the bill for my knee MRI from 2005 so I can claim it against my HSA.”

Three product layers. One compounding foundation.

3

Discover patterns across domains

Reuse the same graph and agent primitives for security incidents, investigations, and operational vigilance.

Platform: one intelligence layer, multiple vertical products.
